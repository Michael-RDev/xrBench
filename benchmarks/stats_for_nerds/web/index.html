Read timings in context.

Total execution includes JNI staging, native forward, and output copy. Native forward includes delegate staging. GPU timing is the sum of operation timestamp intervals, not critical-path latency. These scopes overlap; do not add them. Unavailable counters cannot establish whether a layer is memory- or compute-bound.
